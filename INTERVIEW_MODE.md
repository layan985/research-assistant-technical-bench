# Interview mode

After each exam, spend 10 minutes answering these aloud without notes:

1. What was the first data-integrity risk you checked?
2. Which assumption could change the substantive estimate?
3. How did you know your merge did not multiply rows?
4. What is your unit of observation before and after processing?
5. Why is your inference procedure appropriate?
6. What would you change at 50M rows instead of 5M?
7. Which check would you hand to a coauthor before trusting the result?
8. If your answer differs from the reference output, how do you determine whether you found a benchmark bug or made one?

The point is to train explanation under the same pressure as execution.
