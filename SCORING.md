# Scoring architecture

Every exam is scored out of 100.

- **Correctness — 50:** joins/estimates/predictions/metadata and required outputs.
- **Reproducibility — 15:** one-command rebuild, seeded randomness, stable paths.
- **Efficiency — 15:** runtime/memory appropriate to the task; no obviously wasteful strategy.
- **Documentation — 10:** exclusions, assumptions, variable definitions, concise README.
- **Research judgment — 10:** catches design/statistical traps rather than merely producing files.

## Critical-fail conditions

A grader may set `critical_fail=true` for any of:

- fabricated or manually altered results not generated by code;
- undisclosed sample exclusions;
- duplicate/key explosion that materially changes the sample;
- inferential specification that contradicts the prompt (for example, unclustered SEs where clustering is required);
- data leakage from held-out labels into the training features;
- destructive modification of raw inputs.

Time is reported separately. Finishing after 90 minutes does not erase technical credit, but the scoreboard marks the attempt `OVERTIME`.
